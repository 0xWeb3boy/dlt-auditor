# Validation Card

## Metadata

- ID: `bor-2017-02-13-bor-storage-e23e86921`
- Bug family: `input_validation_and_invariant_enforcement`
- Bug class: `improper-integrity-validation`

## What Confirmed The Issue

- swarm/network/depo.go now hashes req.SData and compares it to req.Key before proceeding.
- The new mismatch branch logs the invalid chunk and ignores the store request, tightening handling of untrusted peer input.

## What Could Have Invalidated It

- Do not flag if the value is produced only by trusted local code and cannot be influenced across a protocol, RPC, or persistence boundary.
- Do not treat as exploitable if an earlier mandatory validation step rejects the malformed input before the sensitive sink.

## Severity Guidance

- Expected impact band: integrity
- Expected severity band: medium

## False-Positive Cautions

- No test or exploit evidence shows that mismatched chunks were previously persisted, propagated, or usable by an attacker.
- No patch evidence proves a concrete confidentiality, integrity, or availability impact beyond accepting invalid data.
