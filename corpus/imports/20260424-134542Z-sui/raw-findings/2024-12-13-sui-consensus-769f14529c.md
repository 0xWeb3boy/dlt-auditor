## How It Was Fixed

The change introduces pending writeback-cache transaction accounting into overload handling, adds threshold configuration, exposes checkpoint watermark state needed for liveness-aware decisions, and wires the commit-described consensus pause to the backpressure condition.

# Why It Matters

1. Limits growth of pending writeback-cache transaction work.

2. Gives RPC callers a retryable overload response.

3. Connects backpressure to checkpoint progress state.

4. May improve validator availability under load.

5. Does not prove a prior security vulnerability.
