## Before/After Behavior

Before the patch, `load_type` directly matched on `TypeTag` and recursively called back into `self.load_type` for nested structures such as vectors. After the patch, `load_type` delegates to `load_type_impl(type_tag, &mut TypeSize::for_type_traversal())`. Before the patch, `abilities` directly matched on `Type`; after the patch, it delegates to `abilities_impl` with a fresh `TypeSize` traversal state and the recursive implementation uses `type_size.enter_type(...)`. The evidence also shows removal or relocation of prior helper code around layout depth/count checks and `checked_subst`, but it is not enough to state precisely how those paths behave after the patch.

# Root Cause

The supported root cause is inconsistent or incomplete traversal accounting in recursive VM type-processing helpers. A stronger claim that this caused an exploitable unbounded-recursion vulnerability is not established by the provided evidence.
