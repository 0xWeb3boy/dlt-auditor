## Missing Property

- Missing property: `full-bid-affordability-gating`

## Violated Invariant

- Invariant: Txpool admission, forwarding, proposal selection, block policy, and execution must agree on the affordability predicate or remove transactions that fail the later stronger predicate.

## Trust Boundary

- Boundary: external transaction -> txpool admission/forwarding -> proposal builder

## Attack Surface

- Entrypoint type: txpool insert and forwarding path
- Sensitive sink: tracked txpool capacity and block proposal construction

## Impact Pattern

- Primary impact: txpool-denial-of-service
- Secondary impact: block-building-starvation
- Severity guide: high

## Short Reusable Lesson

- Txpool insertion used a cheaper base-fee-only balance check, while proposal/block policy used the full gas bid. Attackers could fill tracked pools with transactions that looked affordable at insert time but could never pass proposal validation. Tighten txpool insertion and forwarding to use the full type-aware max-gas-cost helper, and evict or demote transactions that fail static affordability during proposal building.
