# Validation Card

## Metadata

- ID: `monad-c4-2025-09-h-04`
- Bug family: `state_machine_and_lifecycle_consistency`
- Bug class: `order-dependent-delegation-state-mismatch`

## What Confirmed The Issue

- Evidence 1: The report gives a two-transaction witness where X is evaluated before Y in txpool order but after Y in block-policy delegated prepass semantics.
- Evidence 2: The local contest block policy globally sets account_balance.is_delegated before per-transaction reserve effects.
- Evidence 3: Current upstream tests and accounting include a delegation_before_first_txn coordinate in block fee tuples.

## What Could Have Invalidated It

- Compensating control 1: The accounting prepass is purely descriptive and cannot affect earlier transaction classification.
- Compensating control 2: All per-transaction reserve checks are replayed in proposal order with the same delegated status transitions as txpool.

## Severity Guidance

- Expected impact band: consensus-liveness
- Expected severity band: high

## False-Positive Cautions

- Caution 1: No issue if block policy records whether delegation existed before the first transaction and applies ordered semantics
- Caution 2: No issue if txpool and block policy share the same whole-block prepass and reserve rules
- Caution 3: Impact is lower if rejected proposals evict the pair or if the account balance cannot be tuned across the emptying/non-emptying boundary
