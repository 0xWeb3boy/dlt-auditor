# Validation Card

## Metadata

- ID: `monad-c4-2025-09-m-01`
- Bug family: `signature_binding_and_signer_scope`
- Bug class: `signed-message-identity-misbinding`

## What Confirmed The Issue

- Evidence 1: The report describes a two-stage validation gap: cryptographic signature recovery exists, but identity field comparison is missing.
- Evidence 2: The attack scenarios cover PrepareGroup, PrepareGroupResponse, and ConfirmGroup impersonation with attacker-signed messages carrying victim ids.

## What Could Have Invalidated It

- Compensating control 1: The final handler receives a verified author and compares it to every claimed identity before mutating group state.
- Compensating control 2: A separate authenticated transport or membership gate makes claimed identities non-attacker-controlled.

## Severity Guidance

- Expected impact band: unauthorized-message-processing
- Expected severity band: medium

## False-Positive Cautions

- Caution 1: No issue if the router passes the recovered author into every final state mutation and the handler compares it to each claimed identity
- Caution 2: No issue if only authenticated validators can reach the handler and membership is checked before side effects
- Caution 3: Impact is lower if group state is advisory, isolated per connection, or rate-limited without affecting block propagation
